| 7 | 8 Nov | SEBI 2025/79 para 5.3.2 ("entities") vs para 5.1.2 | The ban-period reduction check runs per UCC. Is that the unit the circular means? 5.3.2 says trading "by entities" must reduce FutEq OI, and 5.1.2 nets within a UCC and gross-adds across UCCs - but that is the MWPL utilisation rule, not the ban-period one. The two units are incomparable rather than one being stricter: an entity whose offsetting legs unwind unevenly (UCC A +10,000 -> +5,000, UCC B -10,000 -> -2,000) passes per UCC, since both legs reduced, while the entity net goes 0 -> +3,000. Per-UCC catches a UCC opening a position that another UCC offsets; per-entity catches the uneven unwind. | Per-UCC is implemented as a scope decision, not a claim that it dominates. An entity-level check belongs ALONGSIDE it, not instead of it, using a date-effective UCC -> entity table. Needs the circular, the joint CC SOP 5.3.4 refers to, or the NSE FAQ to say what "entity" means for this rule. | check in source |
| 8 | 8 Nov | NSE Clearing FAQ Q15 vs SEBI 2025/79 paras 5.8.1.4 and 5.8.1.5 | FAQ 15 summarises FPI Category II as individuals at 10% and "non-individuals" at 20%. SEBI puts the sub-category of individuals, family offices AND corporates at 10%, and only the rest of Cat II at 20%. On the FAQ's wording a Cat II corporate or family office gets 20%, double its limit. | Follow SEBI, the primary source. Cat II is two enum members with no bare Cat II, and a Cat II holder can only be categorised through Category.for_fpi_cat_ii(), which refuses an unrecognised sub-category rather than defaulting to the lenient 20%. Regression test in tests/test_entities.py. Worth noticing that the FAQ was right twice this week (Q14 on expiry, Q16 on the penalty) and wrong here: near-primary is not primary. | cleared |
| 9 | 8 Nov | Master Circular Ch 5 cl 4.3.3.4, importing index-futures cl 1.3.5 | Stock entity limits are a share of MWPL, which resets quarterly. An unchanged position can end up over its limit when MWPL is cut. Is that a violation? 4.3.3.4 imports the index rule that a breach caused by a fall in market OI is "passive" and not a violation - but that rule is written about OI falling, not MWPL. | Unresolved. The check reports it as over the limit with cause = passive, so nothing is hidden; whether it is exempt from penalty is the open part. Needs the joint CC SOP or an NSE circular on entity-limit breaches after an MWPL reset. | check in source |
| 10 | 8 Nov | SEBI 2025/79 para 5.8.1.6 | Para 5.8.1.6 asks exchanges and clearing corporations to build a joint SOP to monitor concentrated positions of top entities, at both delta and notional level. Kept as a possible v0.2 feature: the engine already computes per-entity FutEq positions for every scrip, so a top-entities-by-concentration report, as a share of MWPL in delta and notional terms, is a short step. | cleared |

| 12 | 8 Nov | SEBI 2025/79 Annexure 1 para 1.3.2.2.3 vs Master Circular cl 1.3.3.2.3 | #016 decision 2 cited the Master Circular's "gross basis at the level of FPI".
