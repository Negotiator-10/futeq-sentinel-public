2. What is MWPL (market-wide position limit)? Which two quantities set it, what is the floor, and how often is it recalculated?
Ans=MWPL is the most Futeq OI that everyone can hold in one stocks FNO. Free Float is the open shares for trading excluding promoters and owners shares. First it was calculated every month but now it is quaterly.
3. When does a stock enter the ban period, and when does it come out? Why are there two different thresholds instead of one?
Ans=The stock enters the ban period when utimisation exceeds 90% and utilisation is Market Futeq OI/MWPL and it comes out when it is lower than 80%. There are two different threshold because the point gaps makes it stay out once it leaves if there was not gap every day it would flip either side.
4. During a ban, what can a trader still do, and what is a violation? Which two positions are compared, and at what delta?
Ans=During a ban a trader can decrease the exposure they cannot increase if it happens there is a penalty min of 5000 to max of 100000. The two positions are that day's ban vs next day until the ban is removed and at 2pm delta of trading day.
5. What are the position limits for a client, for a trading member's own book, and combined?
Ans= For a client it is 10% of MWPL, for trading member proprietary it is 20% and for combined it is 30%.
6. What is the penalty for a violation, and how is it counted (per what)?
Ans=The penalty for a violation is 5000 to 100000 rupees and it is calculated as 1% of excess value and excess value is calculated as excess qty multiplied by closing price.
7. Which files does NSE give members to monitor all this? Which of them might be public, and which are member-only?
Ans=The client files are member only and public files are Futeq OI of the stocks.
